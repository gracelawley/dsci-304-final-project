# Project Proposal

**Due: Fri 11/13/26 @ 11:59 pm**

A Google document (single spaced, 1-2 pages) that contains the following items:

1.  **Working project title**
2.  **Topic background**
    - Describe the general topic you want to explore.
    - Why is it important to you? Why are you interested in it?
3.  **Research question**
    - A focused, clear question.
    - Complex enough that you will have ample threads to explore and unravel.
    - Can have more than 1 research question, but no more than 3.
    - Avoid questions that could be easily answered with yes or no without doing any statistical modeling.
    - For more guidance on crafting your research question and corresponding hypothesis, see [this article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6322175/).
4.  **Hypothesis**
    - State your hypothesis for your research question.
    - If you have more than 1 research question, write a separate hypothesis for each.
5.  **Dataset choice(s)**
    - If you've already decided which dataset you want to work with, just describe that one. If not, list your topic 2-3 picks.
    - For each dataset mentioned:
      - Link to data source (where to obtain the file)
      - File type (CSV, excel, etc.)
      - List of all variables included
      - Potential outcome variables) to model
      - Any other notes about the dataset that are worth including
    - Each dataset should also meet the following requirements
      - At least 10 columns
      - At least ~1,000 rows
      - At least 1 outcome variable
